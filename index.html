Frequently Asked Questions

What does ScoreDesk actually do?

ScoreDesk reads a CIBIL (or other bureau) credit report PDF and turns the account table, EMIs, balances, and enquiries into a clean Excel file — plus a quick on-screen Risk & Score Dashboard. It's a document-reading and analysis tool, not a bureau or lender.

Is my report uploaded anywhere?

No. The PDF is read entirely inside your own browser using JavaScript — nothing is ever sent to a server. Closing the tab discards everything. This is different from most CIBIL-parsing tools, which upload your file to their servers for processing.

Do you pull my score directly from CIBIL, like a bureau API?

No — ScoreDesk only reads the PDF report you upload yourself; it doesn't connect to TransUnion CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, or CRIF's live systems, and there's no bureau partnership or API behind it. If you already have a report (downloaded from the CIBIL portal, your bank, or a DSA), ScoreDesk just extracts what's already on the page.

Which report formats are supported?

Several common TransUnion CIBIL layouts (self-pull, bank-pull, DSA-pull variants) are supported, plus a best-effort reader for Experian and other bureau exports. If a layout isn't recognized, ScoreDesk falls back to a generic scan and flags the result so you know to double-check it.

Why is my CIBIL Score sometimes not shown?

In a few report layouts, the score is a drawn dial/gauge picture rather than real text in the PDF. Since a picture of "690" can be misread by software as "698", ScoreDesk deliberately doesn't guess at it — it tells you to check page 1 of the PDF directly instead of risking a wrong number.

Is the Risk & Score Dashboard an official bureau score?

No. The repayment history, credit utilization, credit age, enquiry frequency, and credit mix bands are ScoreDesk's own heuristic read of the data already in your report — a quick-glance guide, not TransUnion CIBIL's or any bureau's official scoring methodology. Always verify important decisions against the original PDF.

Is this free, and why do you show ads / ask for donations?

Yes, the tool itself is completely free with no sign-up. SmartRupee is self-funded, so a small AdSense banner and an optional UPI donation help keep it running — neither affects how your report is read, since everything happens locally in your browser either way.

Privacy Policy

ScoreDesk is built so your credit report never has a reason to leave your device:

  • Every PDF you upload is read and parsed entirely inside your own browser using JavaScript — it is never transmitted to any server, including SmartRupee's.
  • If a PDF is password-protected, the password is used locally to unlock it in-browser and is never sent anywhere or stored.
  • Closing or refreshing this tab discards everything that was read — nothing persists after you leave.
  • The optional "Send an Enquiry" and "Leave a Review" forms open WhatsApp / submit only what you type there, and are the only information that ever reaches us.
  • This page shows ads (Google AdSense) which may set their own cookies per Google's policies — that's independent of, and has no access to, anything from your report.

Terms of Use
